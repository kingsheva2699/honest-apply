# Honest Apply: text-only workflow

Use this when your AI chat cannot run local Python or access a repository checkout. It neither runs Honest Apply's checker nor produces a PDF.

1. Paste the job description and only the facts you want the AI to use. Do not include passwords, identity documents, or answers to sensitive questions.
2. Ask the AI to select relevant confirmed résumé bullets verbatim. If it cannot support a requirement from your facts, mark it unknown instead of guessing.
3. Compare each bullet, number, date, company name and answer to your own records. Save or export the reviewed résumé with a local document editor.
4. Open the posting yourself. Copy the reviewed material into the form, verify it, and submit it yourself. Do not ask the AI to sign in, fill live application fields, or submit.
